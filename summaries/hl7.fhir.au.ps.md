# General

AU Patient Summary describes how a short summary of a person’s health information can be shared in Australia. It uses the HL7 FHIR standard to support sharing this information in a consistent way between different healthcare systems.

A patient summary includes key information such as current health conditions, medicines, allergies, and other important clinical details. It provides a snapshot of a person’s health at a point in time, based on the information available.

AU Patient Summary is based on the International Patient Summary and provides a common way to share essential health information when patients move between healthcare services.
