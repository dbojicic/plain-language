# General

AU Core is a set of rules and best practices thatthat support the sharing of healthcare information across Australia. It uses the HL7 FHIR standard to align how data is shared in a consistent way between different healthcare systems. 

AU Core focuses on the health information that is most important for care and is aligned with (Australian Clinical Data for Interoperability [AUCDI](https://sparked.csiro.au/index.php/products-resources/aucdi/). This includes information such as patient details, medicines, conditions, and test results. It provides a common approach that hospitals, general practices, and other healthcare services use to record and share information, even when they use different software systems.
